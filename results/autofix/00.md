# Replay Zap runs
Updated 5 months ago
You may want to replay a previous Zap run. You can do so from Zap history or the Zap editor. Learn more about [how replay works](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/19220226086797/).
[Replay errored Zap workflows](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496241726989-Replay-Zap-runs#tab-content-10) [Replay entire Zap run](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496241726989-Replay-Zap-runs#tab-content-11)
## Replay errored Zap workflows
When a Zap errors due to an [errored status](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/20505304170637-Review-Zap-run-statuses#h_01K7PBKCFTS1856CMG0V7YM97G), you can manually replay it or let Zapier automatically replay it for you.
### Manual replay
With manual replay, you must initiate a replay yourself.
![miscEye icon](https://cdn.zapier.com/storage/photos/335619ab6472fe1fefab18d493cb39bf.png) Note
On the Free plan, manual replay is only available for Zap runs with an errored status. To manually replay Zap runs with other statuses, such as on hold, you need a [paid plan](https://zapier.com/app/billing/plans).
Available on plans:
![A green checkmark](https://cdn.zapier.com/storage/photos/f342bf0b5228dcdc5329dd97411473cb.png)
Free
![A green checkmark](https://cdn.zapier.com/storage/photos/f342bf0b5228dcdc5329dd97411473cb.png)
Professional
![A green checkmark](https://cdn.zapier.com/storage/photos/f342bf0b5228dcdc5329dd97411473cb.png)
Team
![A green checkmark](https://cdn.zapier.com/storage/photos/f342bf0b5228dcdc5329dd97411473cb.png)
Enterprise
#### From Zap history
  1. Go to [Zap history](https://zapier.com/app/history).
  2. Select the **Zap runs** you want to replay. You can use the dropdown menus to filter Zap runs by status, date range, owner, folder, app, or Zap name. 
     * To individually select Zap runs to replay, select the **checkbox** next to each Zap run.
     * To bulk select Zap runs to replay, click the **checkbox dropdown menu** in the upper left and select: **Select shown** to select all the Zap runs on the page. **Select all (5000 at a time)** to select up to 5,000 Zap runs.
  3. In the upper left, click **Play X** (X indicates the number of Zap workflows runs that you've selected to replay).
  4. In the dialog box, click **Replay** to replay the selected Zap runs.
  5. A notification window will open to show that the Zap run is replaying. 
     * Here, you can monitor the status of your replay job.
     * Once it's complete, you'll see a summary of the results.


![Manually replay errors in Zap history](https://cdn.zappy.app/4959d552f4ec38763033d6b218d11b17.png)
#### From the Zap editor
  1. Open the Zap in the [Zap editor](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496291148685).
  2. In the left sidebar, click the **Zap runs icon** ![miscClock](https://cdn.zapier.com/storage/photos/8aeb328e01a50022821101e080183443.png) to expand it.
  3. Select one or more **Zap runs**.
  4. Click **Replay X runs** , where "X" indicates the number of selected Zap runs. A dropdown menu will open.
  5. Click **Errored steps**.


![Manually replay errors in Zap editor](https://cdn.zappy.app/b9d8dab72b0caaee8713c650023715e3.png)
Learn more about [manually replaying Zap workflows that error](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/19220226086797#h_01H9KB37H2E87D8C7YAWBBT476).
### Autoreplay
When you enable [autoreplay](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/19220226086797/#h_01H9KBC1HMW07HTC6EC7NBTENH), Zapier will automatically replay any Zap run with an errored status.
Available on plans:
![A grey X](https://cdn.zapier.com/storage/photos/b6b88934aaa06abf874adf600aa39b02.png)
Free
![A green checkmark](https://cdn.zapier.com/storage/photos/f342bf0b5228dcdc5329dd97411473cb.png)
Professional
![A green checkmark](https://cdn.zapier.com/storage/photos/f342bf0b5228dcdc5329dd97411473cb.png)
Team
![A green checkmark](https://cdn.zapier.com/storage/photos/f342bf0b5228dcdc5329dd97411473cb.png)
Enterprise
#### Enable autoreplay for your entire account
This will automatically replay any errored Zap runs across your entire Zapier account. It will not replay safely halted Zap runs. You must be an account super admin or owner to enable autoreplay.
  1. Go to the _Zap Runs_ tab in your [Zap History](https://zapier.com/app/history).
  2. In the upper right, click to toggle the **Autoreplay** switch on.


![Autoreplay switch in Zap history](https://cdn.zappy.app/312b6c73bd6ce577b014b889c4cf85e9.png)
#### Enable autoreplay for an individual Zap
Members of your Zapier account can enable autoreplay on a per-Zap basis for any Zap they own. This overrides the account-wide autoreplay settings.
  1. Open your Zap in the Zap editor.
  2. In the left sidebar, click the **advanced settings icon**.
  3. Click the **Autoreplay override?** dropdown menu to expand it, then select an option for this Zap: 
     * **Use account setting** : the Zap will autoreplay according to the current account-wide setting. The account-wide setting is shown in parentheses.
     * **Always replay** : the Zap will always autoreplay when there’s an error.
     * **Never replay** : the Zap will never autoreplay when there’s an error.


![Enabling autoreplay for individual Zap in Zap editor](https://cdn.zappy.app/c2124df621a6881c7efa3e530a2f88c7.png)
### Limitations
  * In Teams and Enterprise accounts, you must be an account super admin or owner to enable account-wide autoreplay.
  * In some configurations, Human in the Loop steps will not autoreplay. Learn more about [Human in the Loop replay limitations](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/38838306755981).


## Replay entire Zap run
![Replay entire Zap in Zap editor](https://cdn.zappy.app/6c0a0057b1508953e86dd00fb8624bd1.png)
You can replay all steps in a completed Zap run. Unlike replaying errors, when you replay all steps, the Zap will replay every single step in the Zap, including the trigger and all previous steps, regardless of their [run status](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/20505304170637-Review-Zap-run-statuses). If you edit and re-publish your Zap, you can still select a Zap run, and Zapier will use that trigger data to replay the Zap from its current published workflow.
Available on plans:
![A grey X](https://cdn.zapier.com/storage/photos/b6b88934aaa06abf874adf600aa39b02.png)
Free
![A green checkmark](https://cdn.zapier.com/storage/photos/f342bf0b5228dcdc5329dd97411473cb.png)
Professional
![A green checkmark](https://cdn.zapier.com/storage/photos/f342bf0b5228dcdc5329dd97411473cb.png)
Team
![A green checkmark](https://cdn.zapier.com/storage/photos/f342bf0b5228dcdc5329dd97411473cb.png)
Enterprise
  1. Open the Zap in the [Zap editor](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496291148685).
  2. In the left sidebar, click the **Zap runs icon** ![miscClock](https://cdn.zapier.com/storage/photos/8aeb328e01a50022821101e080183443.png) to expand it.
  3. Select one or more **Zap runs**.
  4. Click **Replay X runs** , where "X" indicates the number of selected Zap runs. A dropdown menu will open.
  5. Click **Entire Zap**.


Learn more about [replaying an entire Zap run](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/19220226086797#h_01HPFFJ31V5D6MD7R6NWARTHSJ).
Tip
You can [modify the trigger data](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/33561391372429) before you replay the entire Zap run.
###  Limitations
  * Your Zap must be turned on before you can replay it.
  * When you replay an entire Zap, it's considered a new Zap run. Any successful steps will count towards your [task usage](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496196837261), even if they were already counted in a previous run.
  * If you want to replay the Zap using an edited Zap workflow, you must publish it first.
  * If you change the trigger app or event, you will not be able to replay the Zap.
  * When you replay an entire Zap run, it will reuse your run's trigger data to generate a new run. 
    * In the [Zap editor](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/16722578092429), it will appear as a separate Zap run in the Zap runs section of the left sidebar. The original run will show a **replay icon** ![actionAutoRenew](https://cdn.zapier.com/storage/photos/6e4e25d2c0b53ab70c19fb91836afe49.png) with the number of replays, and you can navigate between the original and replayed runs from the Zap outline.
    * In your [Zap history](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496291148685-View-and-manage-your-Zap-history), it will appear as a separate item in your list with its own [Zap run details](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/20512774106125-View-specific-Zap-run-details) page.
