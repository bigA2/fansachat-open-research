# How to troubleshoot errors in Zap workflows
Updated 16 days ago
Zap workflows can connect with 9,000+ apps, making it difficult to cover every specific error scenario. However, the following information can help you identify common errors you may encounter and discover solutions.
This guide will cover:
  * How to identify an error.
  * HTTP status codes, including definitions, possible causes, and suggested next steps.
  * How to recover from errors.
  * Troubleshoot Zap workflows.
  * What happens if you do not fix an error.


## How to identify an error
Zapier assigns all Zap runs a [run status](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/20505304170637). You can view these in your [Zap history](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496291148685-View-and-manage-your-Zap-history) and in the [Zap editor](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/16722578092429). If a Zap run is unsuccessful, that does not necessarily mean there is an issue. Learn the difference between these unsuccessful statuses:
  * **Errored** — The run encountered an issue and did not complete. If a Zap errors repeatedly, it will automatically turn off. This article focuses on troubleshooting errored runs.
  * **Safely halted** — The run purposely stopped, usually because a [search step](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496241402253) found no results. Unlike errors, safely halted runs will not turn off your Zap.
  * **On hold** — The run is waiting for an issue to be fixed, typically a disconnected app, reaching your task limit, a plan change, or flood protection. Zapier stores the trigger data, so you can replay the run once the issue is fixed. Learn how to [troubleshoot held runs](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/37454233721869-How-to-troubleshoot-held-Zap-or-step-runs).
  * **Handled error** — A step errored, but a [custom error handler](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/22495436062605-Set-up-custom-error-handling) ran an alternative workflow. The Zap will not turn off automatically.
  * **Scheduled** — The run errored but [Autoreplay](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496241726989) is enabled, so Zapier will automatically retry it.


Learn more about all [run statuses](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/20505304170637) in Zap workflows.
You can identify and troubleshoot errored runs using AI-powered troubleshooting in your Zap history or the Zap editor. This feature explains the issue and provides step-by-step instructions to resolve it.
Zap history
### Review issues in your Zap history
In your [Zap history](https://zapier.com/app/history):
  * Click a Zap run that has an [Errored status](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/20505304170637#h_01K7PBKCFTS1856CMG0V7YM97G). You will be redirected to its [Zap run details](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/20512774106125-View-specific-Zap-run-details) page.
  * Find the **errored step** , then click the **Troubleshoot** tab.
  * Zapier will use AI to create troubleshooting instructions for you to review.


![AI-powered troubleshooting instructions in Zap history](https://cdn.zappy.app/61b63bad0b6965b6c6b6d5f3ed6b7b8c.png)
Zap editor: Zap runs
### Review issues in the Zap editor's Zap runs section
In the [Zap editor](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/16722578092429):
  * Click on the **Zap runs icon** in the left sidebar to expand it.
  * In the list of Zap runs, click the arrow next to a **Zap run** that errored to expand it.
  * Click **Go to step**. This will expand the right sidebar and auto-select the errored step.
  * In the right sidebar, click the **Troubleshoot** tab.
  * Zapier will use AI to create troubleshooting instructions for you to review.


![AI-powered troubleshooting instructions in Zap history](https://cdn.zappy.app/87139a31eb53d4c6e3f2ff7043f0ffc2.png)
Tip
If an outage affects an app used in your Zap, a notification will appear in the affected step. The app's team typically resolves these outages. Contact the app directly for more information or support.
![Example outage notification in a Slack step displayed in the Zap editor](https://cdn.zappy.app/11a07ddedccebfd124f2c69cea3ec766.png)
Zap editor: Zap status
### Review issues in the Zap editor's Status section
The Status section in the editor's left sidebar notifies you when there are any issues with your Zap that will prevent you from publishing it. It appears when you:
  * Edit a [draft of your Zap](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/9693520498445-Create-Zap-drafts-and-versions).
  * Encounter an issue with your Zap after publication.
  * Should [test steps](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/18811411817741-Test-Zap-steps) in your Zap.


To review issues:
  1. In the editor, click the **Status icon**![statusStatus](https://cdn.zapier.com/storage/photos/2a945aa5b8e050070d8c557731b5ddf0.png) in the left sidebar to expand it. 
     * The _number icon_ indicates how many issues the Zap has.
  2. Affected steps will display an _issue notification box_.
  3. Click the **issue notification box** to open the affected step.

Error, warning, and information icons also appear in the top menu of the affected step and in the sub-section of the affected step where the issue is occurring.
Zap editor: Zap's HTTP log
### Review issues in the Zap’s HTTP log
Understanding your Zap’s HTTP log can help you diagnose issues with your Zap workflows. Though it delves into technical details, the HTTP log is an accessible tool for identifying Zap issues.
To find a HTTP log for a Zap step:
  * In your [Zap history](https://zapier.com/app/history), select a **Zap run** that has an [Errored status](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/20505304170637#h_01K7PBKCFTS1856CMG0V7YM97G). You will be redirected to its [Zap run details](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/20512774106125-View-specific-Zap-run-details) page.
  * Find the errored step, then click the **Logs** tab.
  * Zapier will display the: 
    * HTTP status code
    * Error message
    * API endpoint
  * Click the **error log** to open a dropdown menu.
  * In the _Request information dropdown_ menu, you can find detailed log, including: 
    * HTTP method
    * API endpoint
    * Parameters
    * Headers
    * Request body


Use this information to identify the cause of your Zap’s error. ![image](https://help.zapier.com/hc/article_attachments/36938296332685)
![miscEye icon](https://cdn.zapier.com/storage/photos/335619ab6472fe1fefab18d493cb39bf.png) Note
If the step errored as it was missing required information to complete the step, logs will not be available.
Tip
If you cannot read the log, copy and paste it into an AI tool. Then, ask the AI to explain the potential causes for your app's error response.
## HTTP status codes
Zapier connects to your apps to receive and send data. When an app's server responds to a request from Zapier, an [HTTP status code](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/HTTP/Status) is sent to Zapier. When an error occurs with your Zap, these error codes can help pinpoint the issue. You can review error codes when you [test a step](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/18811411817741-Test-Zap-steps) in your Zap or in a specific [Zap run detail](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/20512774106125-View-specific-Zap-run-details).
400 - Bad Request
**Definition**
The server could not understand the request due to invalid syntax.
**Possible causes**
This may happen when there are missing values in required fields, invalid characters in your input, or an incorrectly formatted request.
**Suggested next steps**
Review these articles:
  * [Zap field will not accept your data](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496199127053-Zap-field-won-t-accept-your-data)
  * [Fix "Required field is empty" errors in Zap workflows](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496038661133)


401 - Unauthorized
**Definition**
The request lacks valid authentication credentials.
**Possible causes**
This usually happens when your access token has expired or been revoked, you changed your password in the app, or the app enforces a session policy that periodically invalidates connections.
**Suggested next steps**
  * [Reconnect your app account](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496290788109-Manage-your-app-connections#reconnect-your-app-accounts) in Zapier. Disconnect the app connection, then reconnect it.
  * If the OAuth popup fails or shows an error, try reconnecting in an incognito window or a different browser.
  * For apps managed by a third-party partner, contact the app's support team directly. The issue may be on their side.


403 - Forbidden
**Definition**
The server understood the request but refused to fulfill it.
**Possible causes**
This usually happens when your app account has insufficient permission to complete the request. For example, you cannot edit a record if you have view-only access.
**Suggested next steps**
Check your app connection and account permissions:
  * Confirm your [app account is connected properly](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496258785421-Connect-your-app-accounts-to-Zapier) to Zapier and has the required permissions. You may need to contact your app’s administrator for access and permission.
  * Search for the app’s How to get started page in Zapier’s [Help Center](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us) to find information about how to connect.


404 - Not Found
**Definition**
The server couldn’t find the resource you requested.
**Possible causes**
This usually happens when you are trying to find a record that does not exist in the app.
**Suggested next steps**
Review this article: [Fix "Not found" errors in Zap workflows](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496215423501#h_01JXYNN1Y95ETVFVTH4FEQFPNR).
422 - Unprocessable Entity
**Definition**
The request was well-formatted but contained errors that prevented the server from fulfilling it.
**Possible causes**
This usually happens when you send data, but it’s in the wrong format, or there is missing data that the request requires.
**Suggested next steps**
Check that all Zap fields are entered correctly:
  * All [required fields have values](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496038661133)
  * All values use the right format for each [field type](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496259603341).


You can use [Formatter](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496212590093) to change data formats or add a [default value](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496278339725) as a fallback whenever your mapped fields do not send data.
429 - Too Many Requests
**Definition**
The user has sent too many requests in a short period.
**Possible causes**
This usually happens when you exceed either Zapier’s or an app’s rate limit.
**Suggested next steps**
Review this article: [Rate limits and throttling in Zapier.](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496181445261-Rate-limits-and-throttling-in-Zapier)
500 - Internal Server
**Definition**
The server encountered an unexpected condition that prevented it from fulfilling the request.
**Possible causes**
This is a generic error message that can be caused by a variety of problems in Zapier or with the app you're using.
**Suggested next steps**
  * If the error only happened once or a few times, it is likely a temporary issue. [Replay](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496241726989) the failed runs from your Zap history. You can also enable [Autoreplay](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496241726989#h_01H9KDDTA16928Y0HE3JVNZF3F) to handle these automatically in the future.
  * If the error occurs on every run, check for ongoing issues: 
    * Go to the [Zapier Status page](https://status.zapier.com/) to check if there are any outages or scheduled maintenance.
    * Check the app's own status page. The issue could be specific to the app your Zap connects to.
    * If neither shows issues, contact [Zapier Support](https://zapier.com/app/get-help).


Tip
Not all errors include an HTTP status code. If your Zap fails with a "timed out" message, the app took too long to respond to Zapier's request. Learn more in [Error: The app did not respond in time](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496246377357-Error-The-app-did-not-respond-in-time) and [Error: Soft timeout limit reached](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/41282538488973-Error-Soft-timeout-limit-reached).
## How to recover from errors
After you identify and fix the cause of an error, you can [Replay](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496241726989) the failed tasks so you do not lose data.
To address future issues, you can enable [Autoreplay](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496241726989#h_01H9KDDTA16928Y0HE3JVNZF3F) to automatically retry failed tasks caused by temporary issues, like a brief API outage or a server timeout. You can also use [custom error handling](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/22495436062605-Set-up-custom-error-handling) to set up an alternative workflow that runs when a step fails.

## What happens if you do not fix an error
Your Zap will automatically turn off if 95% of its runs result in errors in the last 7 days. Learn more about why your [Zap isn’t running](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496216132621-Zap-is-not-running).
If your account is on a [Team or Enterprise](https://zapier.com/app/billing/plans) plan, Zapier will send you an email notification to the account owner and provide a [grace period](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496216132621#h_01H91A781J42V8M3H2ZYC7QDNS) before turning off your Zap. Enterprise plan accounts have a 72-hour grace period and Team plan accounts have a 24-hour grace period.
If you have an [Enterprise](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496291989645) account, you can [override the high error ratio setting](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/8496216132621#h_01H91A781J42V8M3H2ZYC7QDNS) for an individual Zap.
